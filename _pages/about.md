I am a Professor in the [Department of Management Information Systems (MIS)](https://www.fox.temple.edu/faculty-research/academic-areas/management-information-systems) in the [Fox School of Business](https://www.fox.temple.edu/) at [Temple University](https://temple.edu).  

I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in cybersecurity and technoogy risk management. My research focuses on various aspects of cybersecurity management and IT governance, including behavioral factors and firm-level causes and consequences of IT security failures, such as data breaches. Journals in which my research appears include _MIS Quarterly_, _Information Systems Research_, _Journal of Management Information Systems_, _Journal of the Association for Information Systems_, _European Journal of Information Systems_, and _MIT Sloan Management Review_, among others. My work and opinions have been cited by numerous popular press outlets, including ABC News, Business Week, The Wall Street Journal, and USA Today.  

I am currently a Senior Editor for _MIS Quarterly_. I previously served as an Associate Editor for _MIS Quarterly_ (2017---2021), _Journal of the Association for Information Systems_ (2019---2023), and _Decision Sciences_ (2014---2018). I received the Outstanding Associate Editor Award from _MIS Quarterly_ (2019) and Reviewer of the Year Awards from both _MIS Quarterly_ (2015) and _Information Systems Research_ (2019). Additional recognitions include the Outstanding PhD Alumni Award from the Fox School of Business, Temple University (2023), and designation as an [Association for Information Systems (AIS) Distinguished Member - Cum Laude (2022)](https://aisnet.org/page/DistinguishedMemberList). 

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Finance and Business Logistics from [Penn State University](https://smeal.psu.edu), a Master of Business Administration from [LaSalle University](https://lasalle.edu/school-of-business/), and a Ph.D. in Business Administration (with a concentration in MIS) from [Temple University's Fox School of Business](https://fox.temple.edu). Prior to my academic career, I was employed by Ford Motor Company as a cost accountant and financial systems analyst.
