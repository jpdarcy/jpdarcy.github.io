I am a Professor in the [Department of Management Information Systems (MIS)](https://www.fox.temple.edu/faculty-research/academic-areas/management-information-systems) in the [Fox School of Business](https://www.fox.temple.edu/) at [Temple University](https://temple.edu). I am also the Howard Gittis Senior Research Fellow.   

I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in cybersecurity and technology risk management. My research examines the behavioral, organizational, and managerial aspects of cybersecurity and IT governance, including employee cybersecurity behavior, the role of corporate governance in cybersecurity, and the causes and consequences of cybersecurity incidents. My research has been published in leading journals, including _MIS Quarterly_, _Information Systems Research_, _Journal of Management Information Systems_, and _Journal of the Association for Information Systems_, among others. My research and insights have also been featured in major media outlets, including ABC News, CBS News, the Associated Press, Bloomberg Businessweek, The Wall Street Journal, and USA Today.  

I am currently a Senior Editor for _MIS Quarterly_. I previously served as an Associate Editor for _MIS Quarterly_ (2017--2021), _Journal of the Association for Information Systems_ (2019--2023), and _Decision Sciences_ (2014--2018). I received the Outstanding Associate Editor Award from _MIS Quarterly_ (2019) and Reviewer of the Year Awards from both _MIS Quarterly_ (2015) and _Information Systems Research_ (2019). Additionally, I received the Outstanding PhD Alumni Award from the Fox School of Business, Temple University (2023) and was named an [Association for Information Systems (AIS) Distinguished Member - Cum Laude (2022)](https://aisnet.org/page/DistinguishedMemberList). 

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Finance and Business Logistics from [Penn State University](https://smeal.psu.edu), a Master of Business Administration from [LaSalle University](https://lasalle.edu/school-of-business/), and a Ph.D. in Business Administration (with a concentration in Management Information Systems) from [Temple University's Fox School of Business](https://fox.temple.edu). Prior to my academic career, I was employed by Ford Motor Company as a cost accountant and financial systems analyst.
